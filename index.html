About

Meet the Hosts

Hi, I’m Colin Scholl, a fourth-year PhD student in experimental psychology at UTEP, with a concentration in bilingualism, language, and cognition and a certificate in quantitative methods. My research focuses on memory for written narrative discourse and bilingual language control, using EEG and eye-tracking. The word on the tip of my tongue is “prerogative,” I always thought it was “per-ogative.”
